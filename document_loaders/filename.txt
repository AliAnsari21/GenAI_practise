The Old Man and the Golden Seed

Once, in a small village surrounded by green fields, there lived a poor farmer named Ravi. He worked hard every day, but his crops were often destroyed by heavy rain or drought.

One morning, while walking through his field, Ravi found a tiny golden seed. An old man nearby told him, “Plant it with care, and never forget to share what you grow.”

Ravi planted the seed and watered it every day. Within a few weeks, a huge tree grew in the middle of his field. Instead of fruits, the tree produced golden grains.

Ravi could have sold all the grains and become rich. But he remembered the old man's words. He gave some grains to every poor family in the village.

The villagers planted the grains, and soon the entire village had healthy crops. Nobody was hungry anymore.

Years later, Ravi became respected not because he was rich, but because he had helped everyone around him.

Moral: True wealth comes from sharing what we have with others.
